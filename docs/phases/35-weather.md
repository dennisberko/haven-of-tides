# Phase 35: Weather

[Previous: Phase 34 - Fishing](34-fishing.md) | [Next: Phase 36 - Ruin exploration](36-ruin-exploration.md)

## Goal

Make one sea route change during a storm.

## Add

- Add clear and storm weather states.
- Show the storm area from outside it.
- Make the storm change ship control in one clear way.
- Make the storm change the result of fishing in that area.
- Show the active weather state on the sea view.

## Use from earlier phases

- Sailing
- Ship damage
- Visible sea opportunities
- Fishing

## Complete when

The player can see a storm, enter it, and feel its clear effect on sailing and fishing.

## Do not add

- Seasons
- A detailed forecast
- Wind simulation
- Random cargo loss
- Strange or cursed storms
- Day and night

## Test

1. Sail the test route in clear weather.
2. Record the ship response and the fishing result.
3. Start the storm state.
4. Sail the same route through the storm.
5. Confirm that ship control and the fishing result change as shown.
6. Leave the storm and confirm that normal rules return.
