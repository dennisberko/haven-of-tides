## Goal

The player can steer the ship through one empty sea area.

## Add

- Move the ship forward under player control.
- Let the player turn left and right.
- Give the ship clear acceleration, top speed, and stopping behavior.
- Stop or redirect the ship at land and the edge of the sea area.
- Keep the ship in view while it moves.

## Use from earlier phases

- Entering the ship
- Player control modes

## Complete when

The player can leave the cove, turn around one island, and return to the cove entrance without losing control.

## Do not add

- Docking
- Wind simulation
- Weather
- Food use
- Other ships
- Combat
- Sea events

## Test

1. Enter the ship at the cove.
2. Sail into the empty sea area.
3. Make a full turn in both directions.
4. Sail around the test island.
5. Return to the cove entrance and stop.
