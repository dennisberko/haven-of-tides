# Phase 10: Salvage

[Previous: visible sea opportunities](09-visible-sea-opportunities.md) | [Next: cargo limit](11-cargo-limit.md)

## Goal

The player can collect one timber lot from the wreck.

## Add

- Show a salvage prompt when the ship is beside the wreck and moves slowly.
- Let the player collect one timber lot with one button.
- Put the timber on the ship.
- Change the wreck to an empty state after collection.
- Keep the collected timber when the player docks.

## Use from earlier phases

- Sailing
- The visible wreck
- Interaction prompts
- Docking

## Complete when

The player can find the wreck, collect its timber one time, and return to the cove with the timber still on the ship.

## Do not add

- Cargo capacity
- Salvage tools
- Damage risk
- Random salvage
- More than one salvage reward
- Wreck reset rules

## Test

1. Sail from the cove to the wreck.
2. Slow the ship and use the salvage prompt.
3. Confirm that one timber lot is on the ship.
4. Try to salvage the empty wreck again.
5. Return to the cove and confirm that the timber remains on the ship.
