## Goal

Make each cannon attack use a limited ship supply.

## Add

- Add an ammunition count to the sea view.
- Use one ammunition unit for each broadside attack.
- Do not fire when the ammunition count is zero.
- Show a low-ammunition warning at two units.
- Let the player load ammunition at a port from weapons and gunpowder cargo.
- Make loaded ammunition use ship cargo space.

## Use from earlier phases

- Broadside attacks.
- Weapons and gunpowder cargo.
- Buying goods at a port.
- Limited cargo space.

## Complete when

Every broadside uses ammunition and the player can load more at a port.

## Do not add

- Different ammunition types.
- Cannon upgrades.
- Free ammunition at sea.
- Prize cannons.
- Crew ammunition tasks.

## Test

1. Leave port with three ammunition units.
2. Fire three broadsides.
3. Confirm that the count reaches zero.
4. Try to fire again and confirm that no shot occurs.
5. Dock, load ammunition from cargo, and fire again.
