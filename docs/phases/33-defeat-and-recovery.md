# Phase 33: Defeat and recovery

[Previous: Phase 32 - Crew injuries](32-crew-injuries.md) | [Next: Phase 34 - Fishing](34-fishing.md)

## Goal

Let the player continue after ship defeat.

## Add

- Detect when the ship is defeated.
- End the current encounter.
- Return the player to the cove or a known port.
- Remove a clear, limited part of the cargo and ammunition.
- Keep the ship damaged and the crew injured.
- Show all losses on one result screen.

## Use from earlier phases

- Cargo
- Ammunition
- Ship damage
- Heat
- Crew injuries
- Cove storage
- Salvage

## Complete when

Ship defeat returns the player to safety with clear losses. The saved game remains playable, and salvage gives the player a recovery path.

## Do not add

- Debt or loans
- Loss of the main ship
- Permanent loss of port access
- Loss of all cargo or money
- A new recovery activity
- Automatic save deletion

## Test

1. Leave the cove with cargo and ammunition.
2. Lose a naval fight.
3. Confirm that the result screen lists each loss.
4. Confirm that the player returns to a safe place.
5. Sail to a known wreck and collect salvage.
6. Confirm that the player can repair and continue.
