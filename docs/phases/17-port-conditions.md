## Goal

Let one visible event make a port market different for a short time.

## Add

- Add one temporary port condition: Storm damage.
- Make timber, food, and medicine Valuable while it is active.
- Let only one condition be active at the port.
- Show the condition name, its market effects, and its remaining voyages.
- End the condition after two completed voyages.
- Return the affected goods to their base price states when it ends.

## Use from earlier phases

- Price states.
- Stock and demand marks.
- Buying and selling cargo.

## Complete when

The player can see the condition before a trade and can see its price effects end.

## Do not add

- More port conditions.
- Random hidden market changes.
- More than three changed goods or services.
- A trade journal.

## Test

1. Visit the port while Storm damage is active.
2. Confirm that timber, food, and medicine are Valuable.
3. Confirm that the remaining duration is visible.
4. Complete two voyages.
5. Return and confirm that the base price states are active again.
