## Goal

Make high heat create a clear danger at sea.

## Add

- Add one pirate hunter ship.
- Make the hunter appear when heat is high.
- Show a warning before the hunter reaches the player.
- Make the hunter chase and attack the player.
- Let the player fight or escape.

## Use from earlier phases

- Sailing
- Target inspection
- Naval attack
- Ammunition
- Sail damage
- Heat

## Complete when

High heat can cause one hunter encounter. The player can inspect the hunter, fight it, or escape from it.

## Do not add

- Separate wanted levels
- Nation or faction rules
- Hunter fleets
- Port service refusal
- New combat actions

## Test

1. Set heat below the hunter limit and sail through the test route.
2. Confirm that no hunter appears.
3. Set heat above the limit and sail through the same route.
4. Confirm that the warning and the hunter appear.
5. Escape from the hunter.
6. Repeat the test and defeat the hunter with the existing combat actions.
