# Phase 01: Walking

[Previous: game design](../game-design-ideas.md) | [Next: interaction](02-interaction.md)

## Goal

The player can walk through one small cove.

## Add

- Move the player in four or eight directions.
- Stop the player at water, walls, and large objects.
- Keep the player in view. Add camera movement only if the cove is larger than the screen.

## Use from earlier phases

None. This is the first playable phase.

## Complete when

The player can walk through all open parts of the cove for one minute and cannot walk through blocked parts.

## Do not add

- An interaction button
- Dialogue
- Items or inventory
- Combat
- The ship
- Time or weather

## Test

1. Start at the cove fire.
2. Walk to each edge of the cove.
3. Walk around the shelter and dock.
4. Try to walk into water, walls, and large objects.
5. Confirm that the player does not become stuck or leave the play area.
