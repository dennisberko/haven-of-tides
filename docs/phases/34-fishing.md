# Phase 34: Fishing

[Previous: Phase 33 - Defeat and recovery](33-defeat-and-recovery.md) | [Next: Phase 35 - Weather](35-weather.md)

## Goal

Add a safe way to collect fish during a voyage.

## Add

- Add one visible fishing area at sea.
- Let the player stop and fish in that area.
- Give the player one type of fish.
- Put the fish in normal cargo space.
- Let the player keep, sell, or discard the fish with existing cargo actions.

## Use from earlier phases

- Sailing
- Visible sea opportunities
- Cargo limit
- Buying and selling
- Resident requests

## Complete when

The player can find a fishing area, catch fish, and make a cargo choice.

## Do not add

- A separate fishing minigame
- Rod, net, or trap upgrades
- Rare fish
- Weather effects
- Time effects
- Monster fishing

## Test

1. Sail to the visible fishing area.
2. Catch one lot of fish.
3. Confirm that the fish uses cargo space.
4. Fill the ship and catch another lot.
5. Confirm that the player must choose what to keep.
6. Sell or deliver the fish with an earlier system.
