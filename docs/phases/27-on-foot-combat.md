# Phase 27: On-foot combat

[← Phase 26: Boarding](26-boarding.md) | [Phase 28: Surrender →](28-surrender.md)

## Goal

Let the player fight one defender during boarding.

## Add

- Put one hostile defender on the target deck.
- Add one short-range cutlass attack for the player.
- Give the player and defender a small health meter.
- Let the defender move close and use one clear attack.
- Add clear hit, damage, and defeat feedback.
- Keep the fight short and use no gore.

## Use from earlier phases

- Walking and collision.
- Interaction input.
- The compact boarding deck.

## Complete when

The player and one defender can damage each other, and the player can defeat the defender.

## Do not add

- Pistols.
- Dodging or parrying.
- More defenders.
- Officer abilities.
- Surrender.
- Prize actions.

## Test

1. Board the target ship.
2. Move into and out of the defender's attack range.
3. Take one hit and confirm the player damage feedback.
4. Use the cutlass and confirm the defender damage feedback.
5. Defeat the defender and confirm that the fight ends.
