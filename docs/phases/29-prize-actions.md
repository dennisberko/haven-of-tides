## Goal

Let the player choose a limited reward after a boarding victory.

## Add

- Open one prize screen after all defenders surrender or are defeated.
- Offer visible cargo, usable cannons, repair materials, and trade records.
- Make each selected prize use one prize action.
- Show the available prize actions before the first choice.
- Reduce the available actions when hull condition is low.
- Block a cargo prize when the ship has no cargo space.
- Treat taken cannons as cargo that the player can sell.
- Let trade records update one port entry in the trade journal.

## Use from earlier phases

- Boarding victory.
- Cargo space.
- Hull condition.
- Repair materials.
- The trade journal.

## Complete when

The player must choose which prizes to take and cannot take every prize.

## Do not add

- Ship capture.
- Ransom or prisoners.
- Cannon modules.
- Story clues.
- Crew injuries.
- Heat changes.

## Test

1. Win one boarding fight.
2. Confirm that the screen shows four prize types and a clear action limit.
3. Take one cargo prize and confirm that it uses cargo space.
4. Use all prize actions and confirm that no more prizes can be taken.
5. Leave and confirm that the selected prizes remain on the player ship.
