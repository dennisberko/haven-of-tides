## Goal

The player can understand a good's price before a trade.

## Add

- Give each shown good one price state: Cheap, Normal, or Valuable.
- Show the state beside the good in the trader view.
- Use a fixed price for each state.
- Show the money change before the player confirms a trade.
- Give the port and cove fixed price states for the test good.

## Use from earlier phases

- Buying and selling
- Money
- Cargo lots and cargo limits
- Travel between the port and cove

## Complete when

The player can identify the price state, see its price, and confirm that the trade uses that price.

## Do not add

- Stock or demand marks
- Temporary port conditions
- Hidden or random price changes
- A trade journal
- Price history or graphs
- Negotiation

## Test

1. Open the trader view at the port.
2. Confirm that the test good is Cheap and has the correct buy price.
3. Buy one lot and sail to the cove.
4. Confirm that the good is Valuable and has the correct sell price.
5. Sell the lot and confirm the money change shown before the sale.
