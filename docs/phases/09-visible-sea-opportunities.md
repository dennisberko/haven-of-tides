## Goal

The player can see a wreck during travel and choose to change course.

## Add

- Place one wreck away from the direct route to the port.
- Show wreckage or smoke before the ship reaches it.
- Keep the wreck visible from enough distance for a course choice.
- Show a simple wreck marker when the player is near it.

## Use from earlier phases

- Sailing
- Waypoint direction

## Complete when

The player can see the wreck while following the port waypoint, leave the route, and reach the wreck.

## Do not add

- Salvage collection
- Random events
- Other ships
- Event timers
- Reward or threat estimates
- A spyglass

## Test

1. Select the port as the waypoint.
2. Start to sail toward the port.
3. Confirm that the wreck is visible before the ship passes it.
4. Turn away from the waypoint and sail to the wreck.
5. Sail back toward the port.
