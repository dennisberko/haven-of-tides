# Phase 07: Docking

[Previous: sailing](06-sailing.md) | [Next: waypoints](08-waypoints.md)

## Goal

The player can dock at the cove, one island, and one port.

## Add

- Mark a small dock area at each location.
- Show a docking prompt when the ship is close, slow, and correctly placed.
- Let the player dock with one button.
- Put the ship in a fixed safe position after docking.
- Let the player go ashore and return to the ship.

## Use from earlier phases

- Sailing
- Entering and leaving the ship
- Walking
- Interaction prompts

## Complete when

The player can sail to all three docks, go ashore, return to the ship, and sail away.

## Do not add

- Dock fees
- Port services
- Trade
- Fast travel
- Automatic docking from long range

## Test

1. Leave the cove by ship.
2. Dock at the island and go ashore.
3. Return to the ship and sail to the port.
4. Dock at the port, go ashore, and return.
5. Sail home and dock at the cove.
