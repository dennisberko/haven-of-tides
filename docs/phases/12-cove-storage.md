# Phase 12: Cove storage

[Previous: cargo limit](11-cargo-limit.md) | [Next: construction](13-construction.md)

## Goal

The player can move cargo between the ship and one storage place in the cove.

## Add

- Place one storage chest or shed in the cove.
- Open a storage view through interaction.
- Show ship cargo and cove cargo together.
- Let the player move one cargo lot in either direction.
- Save both cargo lists after the player closes the view.

## Use from earlier phases

- Walking
- Interaction
- Docking at the cove
- Cargo slots and cargo lots

## Complete when

The player can store timber, sail away with the freed ship slot, return, and take the same timber back.

## Do not add

- More storage buildings
- Storage upgrades
- Warehouses at foreign ports
- Automatic cargo transfer
- Construction
- Item sorting or filters

## Test

1. Return to the cove with timber on the ship.
2. Go ashore and open cove storage.
3. Move the timber from the ship to storage.
4. Leave the cove, return, and open storage again.
5. Move the timber back to the ship and confirm the slot counts.
