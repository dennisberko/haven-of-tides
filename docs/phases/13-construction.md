## Goal

The player can use salvaged timber to make one visible building in the cove.

## Add

- Place one construction site for a storage shed.
- Show its fixed timber cost.
- Count timber in cove storage.
- Let the player confirm construction when enough timber is stored.
- Remove the timber and replace the site with the finished building.

## Use from earlier phases

- Walking and interaction
- Salvage
- Cargo limits
- Cove storage

## Complete when

The player can salvage the required timber, store it, build the storage shed, and see the finished building after leaving and returning to the cove.

## Do not add

- More building choices
- Build time
- Workers
- Building levels
- A passive cove economy
- Taxes or policies

## Test

1. Open the construction site before enough timber is stored.
2. Confirm that construction is not available and the cost is clear.
3. Salvage and store the missing timber.
4. Confirm construction and check that the timber is removed.
5. Leave and return to confirm that the finished building remains.
