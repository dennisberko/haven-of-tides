## Goal

Let the player enter one small ruin and find treasure.

## Add

- Add one ruin entrance on a small island.
- Add one compact ruin area.
- Let the player walk through the ruin.
- Put one treasure reward at the end.
- Put the treasure in cargo space.
- Let the player leave the ruin and return to the ship.

## Use from earlier phases

- Walking
- Interaction
- Docking
- Cargo limit
- Buying and selling

## Complete when

The player can enter the ruin, find the treasure, and carry it back to the ship.

## Do not add

- Tool gates
- Puzzles
- Story clues
- Curses
- Large combat areas
- Procedural ruins

## Test

1. Dock at the ruin island.
2. Walk to the entrance and enter the ruin.
3. Follow the short path to the treasure.
4. Take the treasure and confirm that it uses cargo space.
5. Return to the ship.
6. Sell the treasure at a port.
